interpretation=reachability flips with the policy object alone, so the denial
comes from the NetworkPolicy and not from a broken route, missing DNS, or a
Service misconfiguration. Enforcement is DROP (the client times out) rather
than REJECT (connection refused).

caveat=the policy only appeared to be a no-op once. The cause was a manifest
applied with `kubectl apply -f` that carried no explicit `namespace:` field,
so the object landed in `default` and matched no gateway pod. Every manifest
in k8s/tls-demo/ now sets `namespace` explicitly.
